About me

I'm a Grade 11 student at Jain Vidyalaya IB World School, studying the IB Career-related Programme (IBCP) in 2025–26 after finishing my 10th under CBSE. I take Physics HL and Mathematics, and the programme has pulled me toward more reflective, practical, skill-based learning.

I want to become a Mechatronics and Advanced Robotics Engineer: someone who builds robots that combine mechanical design, electronics, sensors, control systems and AI to work in the real world. The apps and websites on this page are how I practise that mix of hardware thinking and software, one build at a time.

Final reflection

My biggest learning

Responsible decisions need both critical thinking and empathy. Understanding other perspectives, and the wider impact of what I do, matters for my own growth and for society.

My strongest skills now

Critical thinking: I can analyse complex issues like AI ethics, privacy, sustainability and social media before forming a view. Communication and collaboration: I explain ideas clearly, listen actively and work with people who see things differently.

How PPS helps my goals

I'm drawn to electronics, autonomous systems and AGI. PPS taught me that technology needs ethical responsibility, clear communication and global awareness behind it, so the systems I build are both innovative and responsible.
